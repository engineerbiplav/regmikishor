About Me

Precision in Finance,
Purpose in Every Number

I am a qualified ACCA (UK) accountant and MBA graduate with over seven years of progressive experience in financial management, donor compliance, and statutory reporting for international development and conservation programmes.

I specialise in implementing financial controls, managing ERP-based accounting systems, and ensuring compliance with IFRS/NFRS and donor-specific regulations. I am experienced in end-to-end financial processes — including Procure to Pay (P2P), Order to Cash (O2C), Record to Report (R2R), and Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) — as well as audit coordination and tax management.

Currently based in Kathmandu, I am adept at liaising with internal and external auditors, donors, and government authorities to ensure transparent, timely, and accurate financial reporting.
